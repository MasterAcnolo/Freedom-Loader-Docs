# Code of Conduct

Freedom Loader aims to provide a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, disability, or experience level. This applies to project spaces including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, social channels, and events.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Welcome differing opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help keep discussions useful and inclusive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sustained disruption, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the project maintainers at **masteracnolo25@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the reporter's privacy. Maintainers may take fair corrective action, including a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces, depending on the circumstances.

For questions, contact the maintainers or open a [GitHub discussion](https://github.com/MasterAcnolo/Freedom-Loader/discussions).

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct.html).
